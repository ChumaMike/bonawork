BonaBlock for Schools · screen options

Fewer things on each screen

These are simpler versions of every screen a person passes through, drawn in the colours the app already uses. Each one shows only the first screen of a phone, because that is what a parent, a gogo or a caretaker sees before scrolling. Tap Choose under the ones you prefer. Your picks are saved, and Claude reads them to build the real thing.

  • One main button per screenThe thing most people came to do is the biggest thing on the page.
  • The answer firstThe first screen says what is going on. Details open when asked for.
  • Big text, big targetsSized for a thumb, sunlight and tired eyes.
  • Plain wordsFingerprints and transaction codes stay behind a Proof button.

1. Home

Today the home page is {{LEN:home}} phone screens long: a photo, a search box, four picture cards, a carousel, how it works and the rule. A first-time visitor has to work out where to tap.

2. A school's page

Today: {{LEN:school}} screens. People arrive here to do one of two things: report something, or see whether a problem they know about is already listed.

3. One problem

The heaviest page today: {{LEN:case}} screens, eleven separate boxes. The one question people bring is "has it been fixed, and who says so?"

4. Reporting a problem

Today it is five steps, each with explanations, hints and notices around the one thing being asked ({{LEN:report}} screens for the describe step alone).

5. After sending a report

Today: {{LEN:receipt}} screens of receipt, including fingerprints and blockchain details. The person needs two things: proof it went through, and the number to follow it with.

6. School staff: reports to handle

Today: {{LEN:admin}} screens of cards, each with several buttons. A principal or secretary opens this between other duties.

7. Independent reviewer: checking a repair

Today: {{LEN:reviewer}} screens per decision. The reviewer stands at the school with a phone and has to answer one thing: is it really fixed?

8. Check a file, inside the app

Today: {{LEN:verify}} screens, with steps, fingerprints and the blockchain panel all visible. The person wants a yes or a no.

9. BonaBlock Verify: the independent blockchain check

This is the separate site that reads Avalanche directly. Today it is {{LEN:verifyweb}} screens and asks an ordinary phone user to paste a block of code, pick between "evidence file", "commitment document" and "acknowledgement", and type a report id. The check itself is strong; the page around it is built for engineers.

10. On every screen: the bottom menu

The menu is on screen all the time, so it sets how busy the whole app feels.

11. On every screen: how much proof to show

The blockchain record is the point of the product, but codes like 0xe6f0a242 mean nothing to most people and make pages look difficult.

12. On every screen: text size

Both samples are shown at the size they would be on a phone.
