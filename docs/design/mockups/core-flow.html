Bona Chain core flow

Six moments from the demo, drawn as the screens will look on a phone. Press the buttons inside the screen to move the case forward, or jump with the numbered moments. The trail beside it updates as you go.

    This is a design mock-up. The names, amounts, times and proofs are sample data for one invented case at an invented school. Photos in the product are AI-generated illustrations and are labelled so. Everything runs on the Avalanche Fuji test network: no real money moves, and Bona AI never approves, verifies or authorises anything.
