### Observed Fuji gas price

Read from the public Fuji RPC on 2026-11-17 at block 61213108 (read-only, no transaction sent):

- base fee: **10 wei** per gas unit
- suggested priority fee: **150 wei** per gas unit
- total: **160 wei** per gas unit (1 nAVAX = 1,000,000,000 wei)

At that price the deployer's whole gas bill is **488,164,000 wei** (0.000000000488164 AVAX). Gas is negligible: the deployer's real requirement is the **0.15 AVAX** of top-ups plus the 0.01 AVAX margin `fund:fuji` insists on, so **0.16 AVAX** in the deployer is enough at this price.

This is a single observation. The base fee rises when the network is busy.
